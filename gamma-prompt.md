## 2. Fare Cap Line
Is the TTC's 47 trip fare cap worth chasing?
Pay as you go never costs more than $155.10 a month. The Adult 12 Month Pass is $143.00 a month. Slide your trips and see which one wins.
alenzacodes.github.io/ttc-fare-cap

## 3. FHSA Head Start
How much faster does a First Home Savings Account get you to a down payment than a TFSA?
Move the levers and watch the tax refund do the work, inside the $8,000 a year and $40,000 lifetime limits.
alenzacodes.github.io/fhsa-head-start

## 4. Canada's Rate Cycle, 2015 to 2026
Did higher rates actually cool inflation?
Bank of Canada data, one window picker, every chart updates. Inflation peaked at 8.1% in June 2022.
